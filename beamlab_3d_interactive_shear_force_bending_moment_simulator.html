About BEAMLAB 3D — Structural Analysis Engine

Euler-Bernoulli Direct Stiffness FEM Engine

Unlike simplified demos that rely on hardcoded formulas, BEAMLAB 3D discretizes every beam into 50 Hermite Cubic Finite Elements (102 Degrees of Freedom). Each node tracks both vertical translation v(x) and rotational slope θ(x).

Consistent nodal load vectors are assembled exactly from analytical fixed-end actions for point loads, partial or full uniformly distributed loads (UDL), trapezoidal/triangular uniformly varying loads (UVL), and concentrated couples.

Determinate & Indeterminate Accuracy

Because the global stiffness matrix [K]{d} = {F} enforces both static equilibrium (ΣFy = 0, ΣM = 0) and kinematic slope/deflection compatibility simultaneously, the engine solves both statically determinate beams (Simply Supported, Cantilever, Overhanging) and statically indeterminate beams (Fixed-Fixed, Multi-Span Continuous) with textbook precision.

Internal Shear Force V(x) and Bending Moment M(x) are subsequently evaluated across 501 high-resolution station points using exact equilibrium integration so jump discontinuities at point loads and applied moments remain razor-sharp.
